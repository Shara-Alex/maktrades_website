About MAK Trades

Practical solutions. Professional workmanship.

MAK Trades and Fittings (Pty) Ltd is a Johannesburg-based renovation and property-maintenance business providing reliable, practical workmanship for homes, rental properties, sectional-title schemes, offices and small commercial spaces.

Led by Newton Makaripe, we handle projects ranging from a single repair or door installation to kitchen and bathroom upgrades and complete interior refurbishments.

We believe in neat finishes, clear quotations and professional service from the first site visit through to completion.
